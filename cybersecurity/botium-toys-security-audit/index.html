Botium Toys: Internal Security Audit

Assessed a retailer's security program against 14 controls and the PCI DSS, GDPR and SOC 2 requirements, and wrote a prioritised remediation plan.

S

Situation

Botium Toys, a small U.S. toy retailer, was growing its online store, taking card payments and selling to customers in the E.U. Its IT department managed a wide range of assets but had never audited its security program.

T

Task

As the internal auditor, assess current controls and compliance, identify gaps, and recommend what the IT manager should prioritise to reduce risk and meet the PCI DSS, GDPR and SOC 2 requirements.

A

Action

  • Reviewed the audit scope, asset inventory and risk assessment.
  • Assessed 14 administrative, technical and physical controls, recording each control's type and the evidence for my answer.
  • Checked 12 compliance best practices across PCI DSS, GDPR and SOC 1/2.
  • Mapped every finding to a NIST CSF function and grouped the recommendations into three priority tiers.
R

Result

  • Found 9 of 14 controls missing, including least privilege, encryption, backups, disaster recovery and intrusion detection, and rated overall risk at 8 out of 10.
  • Showed that two gaps, access control and encryption, cause most of the PCI DSS and GDPR failures, so fixing them first gives the biggest reduction in risk.
  • Delivered a report ready for stakeholders, with a prioritised remediation roadmap.
